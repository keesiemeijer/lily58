## Dongle

### Flashing

1. Flash a `settings_reset` to all three devices: the dongle, the left half and the right half. Don't skip one.
2. Flash the normal firmware on all three again, with the dongle being last. Turn of the device after flashing.
3. Power the dongle first, then the left half, then the right half. Wait for each to connect before starting the next. This makes the left half peripheral 1 and the right half peripheral 2.
4. Unplug the dongle from the computer and plug it in again.
